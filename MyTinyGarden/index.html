About the game

My Tiny Garden is a cozy gardening game from Moonstream Tech, and it is in development right now.

Plant seeds in pots, water them and harvest what grows. Fill your crates, stock the stand and sell to the customers who stop by — set your own prices and decide whether to accept their offers. Spend what you earn on new seeds, bigger pots and decorations, and watch a small patch turn into your dream garden.

Everything is saved on your phone. There is no account, no sign-in and nothing to buy.

What's inside

Everything below is in the current build

  • 01

    Twelve crops to grow.

    Tomatoes, cucumbers, peppers, pumpkins, strawberries, carrots, lettuce and watermelons, plus daisies, tulips, lavender and roses.

  • 02

    Crops that level up.

    Every harvest gives XP. Levelled crops grow faster, and at level 10 every harvest is doubled.

  • 03

    Your own stand.

    Carry crates to the stand, set your prices and bargain: accept a customer’s offer with ✓ or turn it down with ✕.

  • 04

    Compost and fertilizer.

    Turn extra or rotten produce into small, medium and large bags of fertilizer for a chance at double harvests.

  • 05

    Pots, planters and crates.

    Round pots, long pots, wooden planters and self-watering pots — place, rotate and move them anywhere on the grid.

  • 06

    Decorate the garden.

    Lamps, lanterns, a lily pond, a flower arch, benches to sit on, a gnome, a scarecrow, a sleeping cat, beehives and more.

  • 07

    Day and night.

    Watch the light change over your garden while your plants grow.

  • 08

    Make it yours.

    Choose your gardener’s look, hair, eyes and hat, grow the garden to more tiles and check the in-game guide and statistics.

  • 09

    Free to play.

    Supported by ads. English and Turkish, switchable in the settings.

How do I start growing?

Buy seeds in the Market, walk up to an empty pot and tap PLANT, then WATER. When the plant is ready, tap HARVEST. Self-watering pots water new seeds for you.

How do I sell my harvest?

Put your produce in a crate (INTO CRATE), carry the crate to the stand and tap ON STAND. Customers will come and buy from it.

How do prices and offers work?

You set your selling prices in the stand’s price panel. Each customer is willing to pay their own amount close to the suggested price. If your price is higher, they make a lower offer that you can accept with ✓ or refuse with ✕. Far above the suggested price (about 2×), nobody will buy.

What does levelling up a crop do?

Each harvest gives that crop XP. Every level shortens its growing time, and at level 10 every harvest is doubled.

How do compost and fertilizer work?

Put crops or rotten produce into the compost bin. When it is ready, take the fertilizer bag and use it on a plant for a limited-time chance at a double harvest. Make sure you have room to carry the extra harvest.

How do I place, rotate or sell items?

Items you buy are placed in the garden: tap ROTATE to turn them and PLACE to confirm. You can sell an item you no longer need once it is empty — but not your last pot.

How do I make the garden bigger?

Open Upgrades. The garden, the stand and the compost bin can all be upgraded, and each crop has its own level.

Will I lose my progress if I change or reset my phone?

Progress is saved only on your phone. It is not uploaded anywhere, so uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes it, and it cannot be moved to a new phone. You can start over with Settings → Reset progress (tap twice to confirm).

Does the game work offline?

Yes. You can play without an internet connection; only ads need one.

How do I report a bug or suggest an idea?

Email moonstreamtech@gmail.com with your phone model, Android version, the game version and a screenshot or video if you can.

Scope

This policy explains how My Tiny Garden for Android, made by Moonstream Tech (“we”, “us”), handles information. It applies only to this game; the general Moonstream Tech Privacy Policy covers the studio as a whole.

Data on your device

Your progress — money, crops and their levels, pots, crates, decorations, your gardener’s look, statistics and settings such as sound and language — is saved only in the game’s local storage on your phone. It is never sent to us, and we cannot see it. It is removed when you reset your progress in the game’s settings, clear the app’s data or uninstall the game. Because it is not backed up by us, it cannot be restored or moved to a new phone.

Data we collect

None. My Tiny Garden has no accounts, no sign-in, no online leaderboard, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no crash reporting. We do not run a server for this game and do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. The only third party that receives data is Google, for advertising, as described below.

Advertising and consent

My Tiny Garden is free to play and is supported by ads from Google AdMob. The game shows a banner ad. Future versions may also include optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch and occasional full-screen (interstitial) ads at natural breaks; this policy covers those formats as well.

When an ad is requested or shown, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process, on Google’s behalf:

  • your device’s advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • your IP address, from which an approximate (coarse) location can be derived
  • device and app information such as model, operating system, language, app version and network type
  • ad interactions such as impressions, clicks and whether a rewarded ad was completed
  • diagnostic data such as SDK errors and performance information

This data is used to deliver, measure and limit the frequency of ads, to prevent fraud and, where you allow it, to personalise ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your choice with Google’s consent form (User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads are shown. If you do not consent, Google may still show non-personalised ads, which use contextual information and limited data for measurement and fraud prevention.

The data collected by AdMob is controlled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. See also: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Third-party services

  • Google AdMob and the User Messaging Platform (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd.) — ads and consent. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play — downloads, updates and, if you choose, Play’s own backup of app data, governed by Google’s policies.

Data processed by Google may be transferred to and stored in countries outside your own, including the United States, under the safeguards Google provides.

Permissions

  • Internet access — to load ads and the consent form.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID, added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK) — to deliver and measure ads.

The game does not access your contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone or location.

Retention and deletion

We do not keep any data about you. Data on your phone stays until you reset progress, clear the app’s data or uninstall the game. Google keeps advertising data according to its own retention periods, described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR, the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. You can also:

  • reset your advertising ID or delete it in your phone’s Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads)
  • uninstall the game, which removes all data stored on your device
  • email us at moonstreamtech@gmail.com to exercise any of your rights

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Children

My Tiny Garden is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us at moonstreamtech@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy when the game changes, for example when a feature or service is added. The “Last updated” date will change and, for significant changes, we will let you know in the game or on this page. The Moonstream Tech Privacy Policy applies to all our games and complements this policy.

Contact

Moonstream Tech · moonstreamtech@gmail.com. We aim to respond within 2–3 business days, and to privacy requests within 30 days at the latest.
