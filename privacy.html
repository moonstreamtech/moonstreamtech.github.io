Moonstream Tech (“we”, “us”, “our”) is an independent studio that makes mobile games and apps. This policy explains, for all of our apps and this website, what information we and the services we use may collect, why, and the choices you have.

Not every app collects everything described here. This is a studio-wide policy that covers every service any of our apps may use. Many of our games collect nothing at all. To see exactly what a specific game or app does — and does not — collect, please visit that game’s own page on this website and read its privacy section, listed under App-specific policies.

Who we are

Moonstream Tech is the developer and data controller for the apps published under the Moonstream Tech name on Google Play and for moonstreamtech.com. You can reach us at moonstreamtech@gmail.com.

Where an app’s own privacy policy is more specific than this one, the app’s policy applies to that app. In all other respects, this policy applies.

App-specific policies

Each game has its own privacy policy that lists exactly which data it handles, which services it uses and how to delete your data. Please check the page of the game you play:

  • Last Tile — Privacy Policy
  • Mozai — Privacy Policy
  • Ribbo: Pond Hopper — Privacy Policy (and account deletion)
  • My Mini BBQ — Privacy Policy
  • My Tiny Garden — Privacy Policy

The Data safety section of each app’s Google Play listing also summarises what that app collects and shares.

Information we may collect

Depending on the app and the features you use, the following categories of information may be collected by us or by the services we use on our behalf.

Information you give us

  • Account information — if an app offers sign-in (for example with Google) and you choose to use it: an account identifier, your email address and basic profile details provided by the sign-in service, such as your name and profile picture.
  • Game profile — a display or player name (chosen or generated), scores, rankings and similar game records used for leaderboards or cloud features.
  • Messages — the content of emails you send us, your email address and anything you attach, such as screenshots.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and app identifiers — such as the advertising ID, an app-specific or installation ID, or an anonymous account ID.
  • Device and technical information — device model, operating system and version, language, time zone, app version, network type and your IP address (from which a coarse, city- or country-level location may be derived). Our apps do not collect precise GPS location.
  • Usage and interaction data — such as ad impressions and clicks, whether a rewarded ad was completed, and in-app events needed for a feature to work.
  • Diagnostics — crash logs and performance data, if an app uses a crash-reporting or diagnostics service.

Purchase information

  • If an app offers in-app purchases or subscriptions: the product purchased, price and currency, country, purchase date, order or transaction ID and subscription status. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play — we never receive your card or bank details.

Information stored only on your device

  • Most of our games save progress, unlocked items and settings only in local storage on your phone. This data is not sent to us, and it is removed when you clear the app’s data or uninstall the app.

We do not collect your contacts, photos, files, messages, microphone or camera input, and we do not ask for sensitive information such as health, religious or financial data.

How we use information

  • to provide the app and its features — saving progress, leaderboards, accounts, cloud features and purchases
  • to show ads that keep our games free, and to measure them and limit how often they appear
  • to verify purchases, restore them on a new device and prevent fraud
  • to keep our services secure, fair and free of cheating or abuse
  • to fix bugs, understand crashes and improve performance
  • to answer your questions and support requests
  • to comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it to build profiles about you ourselves.

Legal bases

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or Türkiye, we rely on the following legal bases under the GDPR, the UK GDPR and the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK):

  • Performance of a contract — to provide the features you use, such as an account, leaderboard or purchase.
  • Consent — for personalised advertising and any other processing that requires it. You can withdraw consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing.
  • Legitimate interests — to keep our apps secure and fair, prevent fraud, fix problems and show non-personalised ads that fund free games.
  • Legal obligation — for example, keeping records required by tax or consumer law.

Services we use

Our apps may use some of the following third-party services. Each processes data under its own privacy policy, as a service provider acting on our behalf or as an independent controller. Which of them a given app uses is listed in that app’s own policy.

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd.) — banner, interstitial and rewarded ads. Privacy Policy
  • Google User Messaging Platform — the consent form for ads, where required by law.
  • Google Play services, Google Sign-In and Google Play Billing — app delivery and updates, sign-in with a Google account, and payments for in-app purchases. Privacy Policy
  • Google Firebase — such as Authentication (including anonymous accounts), Cloud Firestore for leaderboards and cloud data, and, where used, Crashlytics or Analytics. Privacy and Security in Firebase
  • Supabase (Supabase Inc.) — database and authentication for accounts, player names and leaderboards. Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.) — manages in-app purchases and subscriptions: verifies receipts with Google Play, keeps track of what you have unlocked and restores purchases. It may process an app user ID, purchase history and device information. Privacy Policy
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) and Vercel (Vercel Inc.) — hosting for this website and for web versions of our games.
  • Google Fonts — the typeface used on this website.
  • Gmail (Google) — our support email.

Advertising and consent

Many of our games are free and supported by ads from Google AdMob. When an ad is requested or shown, Google may collect your advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interactions to deliver ads, measure them, limit their frequency, prevent fraud and — if you allow it — personalise them. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, our apps ask for your consent with Google’s consent form before personalised ads are shown. If you decline, you may still see non-personalised ads. Where an app offers a “Privacy options” button, you can change your choice at any time. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Rewarded ads are always optional: you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game reward.

In-app purchases

Some apps may offer optional in-app purchases or subscriptions. Payments are processed by Google Play Billing under your Google account; we only receive confirmation of the purchase, not your payment details. We may use RevenueCat to validate purchases, keep your unlocked content in sync and restore purchases on a new device. Refunds follow Google Play’s refund process.

Accounts and leaderboards

Playing our games never requires an account. Some games offer optional online features such as leaderboards, which may use an anonymous account (for example Firebase Authentication) or sign-in with Google (for example through Supabase). Leaderboards show your display name and scores to other players; your email address is never shown.

Where an app creates an account, you can delete it and the data linked to it — inside the app where available, or by emailing us. Instructions are on each game’s page (for example, Ribbo account deletion).

How information is shared

  • With service providers listed above, only as needed to run the app features they provide.
  • With other players — only what you choose to make public, such as a leaderboard name and score.
  • For legal reasons — if required by law, a court order or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users and our studio.
  • In a business transfer — if an app or the studio is sold or merged, information may transfer to the new owner under the same protections.

We do not sell personal information for money. Advertising partners may use data from ad requests as described in their own policies; you can limit this with the ad consent and device settings above.

International transfers

Our service providers operate servers in several countries, including the United States and the European Union. When information is transferred outside your country, it is protected by the safeguards these providers offer, such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses, and in line with Article 9 of the KVKK where it applies.

Retention

  • Data stored on your device stays until you clear the app’s data or uninstall the app.
  • Account and leaderboard data is kept while your account exists and deleted when you delete the account (backups are cleared within 30 days).
  • Purchase records are kept as long as needed to provide your purchases and to meet legal and tax obligations.
  • Support emails are kept for as long as needed to help you, and deleted on request.
  • Third-party services keep data according to their own retention periods.

Security

Data sent between our apps and online services is encrypted with HTTPS. Online databases use access rules so that each player can only read and change what they are allowed to. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and to collect as little as possible.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy
  • correct inaccurate data
  • delete your data and account
  • restrict or object to certain processing
  • receive your data in a portable format
  • withdraw consent at any time
  • not be discriminated against for exercising your rights (for example under California law)
  • lodge a complaint with your data protection authority — in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK)

To use any of these rights, email moonstreamtech@gmail.com. We may ask you to confirm the request from the email address linked to your account, and we reply within 30 days.

Children’s privacy

Our apps are made for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The advertising and online services we use are general-audience services. Parents can use Google Family Link and the ad settings on the device to limit ad personalisation and online features.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

This website

moonstreamtech.com is a static website. We do not use cookies, analytics or tracking on it. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log your IP address and browser information for security, and the font is served by Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads. Links to Google Play and other sites are governed by those sites’ own policies.

Changes to this policy

We update this policy when we publish new apps, add features or start using a new service. The “Last updated” date above will change, and significant changes will also be announced in the affected apps or on their pages.

Contact

Questions or requests about privacy? Email moonstreamtech@gmail.com. We aim to respond within 2–3 business days.
