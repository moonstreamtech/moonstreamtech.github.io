About the game

My Mini BBQ is a cosy idle tycoon about a small BBQ stand that keeps getting bigger.

Hungry drivers honk at your window. Cook their order on the grill, carry it over and hand it out before they run out of patience. Every sale pays for new machines, new dishes and a crew that takes over the busy work — until your little stand runs almost by itself.

Play with one finger. Your progress is saved on your phone — no account, no sign-in. The game is free to play; in-app purchases are optional.

Help & Support

Answers to the most common questions about My Mini BBQ. Can’t find what you need? Email us — we read every message.

Still stuck?

How do I play?

Walk around your stand with the on-screen joystick (on PC: WASD or the arrow keys). Walk up to a machine or counter to use it: pick up what’s cooked, carry it to the window and serve the car that ordered it. Money from each sale goes to upgrades in the Upgrades menu and supplies in the Market.

Why won’t customers order a product I have?

A product is only sold when its machine is installed, you own its sales licence and it is switched on in the menu. If you turn a product off, customers will not ask for it and your stock hand will not order its ingredients.

I ran out of ingredients. What now?

Order more boxes from the Market; they arrive on a pallet and have to be carried into storage. Hire a stock hand to carry them for you — from level 2 they reorder by themselves when a storage drops below the critical level.

Why do customers drive away?

Every car only waits so long. Serve faster, hire chefs and runners, add grill and fryer slots, and upgrade your own speed and carrying capacity. Your star rating shows how well you are doing — more stars bring more customers.

How do I get the free $100?

Tap FREE $100 and watch a short rewarded ad to the end. If no ad is available right now (for example without an internet connection), try again a little later — you will not be charged anything.

Why do I sometimes see a full-screen ad?

An occasional full-screen ad may appear when your shop closes, never during service, and at most once every two minutes.

Will I lose my progress if I change or reset my phone?

Progress is saved only on your phone. It is not uploaded anywhere, so uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes it, and it cannot be moved to a new phone. Permanent purchases and an active VIP membership are tied to your Google account and come back with Restore purchases in the store. You can start over at any time with Settings → Reset progress.

How do I change the language or turn the sound off?

Open Settings from the menu. Sound, graphics quality and language (57 languages) are saved on your device. If the game feels slow on an older phone, choose the lowest graphics setting.

Do I need an internet connection?

Yes. My Mini BBQ needs an internet connection to play. If you lose the connection, the game pauses and continues as soon as you are back online.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases and the VIP membership are paid through Google Play, so refunds follow Google Play’s refund process. You can cancel the VIP membership at any time with Manage subscription in the game’s store or under Payments & subscriptions in the Google Play app.

How do I report a bug or suggest an idea?

Email moonstreamtech@gmail.com with your phone model, Android version, the game version and a screenshot or video if you can.

Scope

This policy explains how My Mini BBQ for Android, made by Moonstream Tech (“we”, “us”), handles information. It applies only to this game; the general Moonstream Tech Privacy Policy covers the studio as a whole.

Data on your device

Your progress — money, shop level, upgrades, crew, stock, menu choices, statistics and settings such as sound and language — is saved only in the game’s local storage on your phone. It is never sent to us, and we cannot see it. It is removed when you reset your progress in the game’s settings, clear the app’s data or uninstall the game. Because it is not backed up by us, it cannot be restored or moved to a new phone.

Data we collect

None through the game itself. My Mini BBQ has no accounts, no sign-in and no online leaderboard. We do not run a server for this game, and we do not sell personal data. Data goes only to Google — for advertising, for app analytics and crash reports (Firebase) and, if you buy something, for in-app purchases — and, where you allow it, to Meta to measure our own advertising, as described below.

Advertising and consent

My Mini BBQ is free to play and is supported by ads from Google AdMob. The game shows a banner ad, an optional rewarded ad (“FREE $100”) that you choose to watch, and an occasional full-screen (interstitial) ad when your shop closes (at most once every two minutes).

When an ad is requested or shown, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process, on Google’s behalf:

  • your device’s advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • your IP address, from which an approximate (coarse) location can be derived
  • device and app information such as model, operating system, language, app version and network type
  • ad interactions such as impressions, clicks and whether a rewarded ad was completed
  • diagnostic data such as SDK errors and performance information

This data is used to deliver, measure and limit the frequency of ads, to prevent fraud and, where you allow it, to personalise ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your choice with Google’s consent form (User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads are shown. If you do not consent, Google may still show non-personalised ads, which use contextual information and limited data for measurement and fraud prevention.

The data collected by AdMob is controlled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. See also: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

App analytics and crash reports (Firebase)

To understand how the game is played and to fix problems, the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd.). They do not identify you by name and we do not use them for advertising to you.

  • Analytics: an app instance ID and, where allowed, your advertising ID; device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version, country); app usage such as sessions, first open, how many days you come back, and game events like finishing the tutorial, reaching a new shop level or watching a rewarded ad; in-app purchases reported by Google Play.
  • Crash reports: when the game crashes or hits an error, technical details such as the error message, the place in the code, device model, operating system and app version.

We use this only to improve the game, its balance and its stability. Firebase data is controlled under the Google Privacy Policy and Privacy and Security in Firebase; Google keeps analytics data for up to 14 months and crash reports for up to 90 days.

Ad measurement (Meta)

We advertise My Mini BBQ on Facebook and Instagram. To find out whether those ads work, the game includes the Meta SDK (Facebook App Events) from Meta Platforms. It does not show ads and does not use Facebook Login: you do not need a Facebook account, and the game never reads your Facebook data.

When it is allowed, the Meta SDK sends Meta:

  • your device’s advertising ID and basic device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version)
  • that the game was installed and opened
  • a few game events: finishing the tutorial, reaching a new shop level and watching a rewarded ad
  • for purchases, the product, price and currency — never your payment details

Meta uses this to measure and improve our ad campaigns and, under its own policies, to improve its ad services. Automatic logging is turned off, so nothing is sent until the game has checked your consent choice. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, data is sent only if you accept storing information on your device and ad measurement in the consent form; you can change that choice at any time in the game’s settings. Everywhere, turning off ad personalisation or deleting your advertising ID in your phone’s settings limits what Meta can link to your device.

This data is controlled by Meta Platforms, Inc. / Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd. under the Meta Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

My Mini BBQ can be played for free. The game’s store offers optional in-app purchases: one-time items (the starter pack, removing ads, double income and decor packs), packs of game cash, and an auto-renewing weekly VIP membership.

All payments are processed by Google Play Billing under your Google account. Your payment method, billing details and purchase history are collected and processed by Google, under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Payments Privacy Notice. We never receive or store your card, bank or other payment details.

When you buy or restore something, Google Play tells the game on your phone:

  • which product was bought and whether the payment is completed or still pending
  • a purchase token and order ID that identify the transaction
  • for the VIP membership, whether the subscription is active

The game uses this only to unlock what you bought, to confirm the purchase with Google Play and to restore it later. The list of items you own is kept in the game’s local storage on your phone; it is not sent to us. Only the product, price and currency of a purchase are reported to Meta for ad measurement, as described above.

As the developer, we can see order records in the Google Play Console — such as the order ID, product, price and currency, country and date. They do not include your payment details. We use them only for accounting, refunds and support.

One-time items and an active VIP membership are tied to your Google account, so you can bring them back on a new phone with Restore purchases. Game cash that has already been added and your progress cannot be restored. If you buy Remove ads or are a VIP member, banner and full-screen ads are no longer shown; rewarded ads stay optional.

The VIP membership renews every week until you cancel it. You can cancel at any time with Manage subscription in the game’s store or in the Google Play app; see how to cancel a subscription on Google Play. Refunds follow Google Play’s refund process.

  • Google AdMob and the User Messaging Platform (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd.) — ads and consent. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Billing (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd.) — payments for in-app purchases and the VIP membership. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd.) — app analytics and crash reports. Privacy and Security in Firebase
  • Meta SDK / App Events (Meta Platforms, Inc. / Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd.) — measuring our Facebook and Instagram ads. Meta Privacy Policy
  • Google Play — downloads, updates and, if you choose, Play’s own backup of app data, governed by Google’s policies.

Data processed by Google and Meta may be transferred to and stored in countries outside your own, including the United States, under the safeguards they provide.

Permissions

  • Internet access — to load ads and the consent form, to send analytics, crash reports and ad measurement, and to reach Google Play for purchases.
  • Google Play billing (com.android.vending.BILLING, added by the Google Play Billing Library) — to offer and complete in-app purchases.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID, added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK and the Meta SDK) — to deliver and measure ads.

The game does not access your contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone or location.

Retention and deletion

The game does not send us any data, so we keep none from it. Data on your phone stays until you reset progress, clear the app’s data or uninstall the game. Order records for purchases are kept by Google Play, and by us for as long as needed to provide your purchases and to meet legal and tax obligations. Google keeps advertising and payment data, and Meta keeps ad measurement data, according to their own retention periods, described in the Google Privacy Policy and the Meta Privacy Policy.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR, the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. You can also:

  • reset your advertising ID or delete it in your phone’s Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads)
  • cancel the VIP membership or review your purchase history in the Google Play app under Payments & subscriptions
  • uninstall the game, which removes all data stored on your device (uninstalling does not cancel a subscription)
  • email us at moonstreamtech@gmail.com to exercise any of your rights

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Children

My Mini BBQ is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us at moonstreamtech@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy when the game changes, for example when a feature or service is added. The “Last updated” date will change and, for significant changes, we will let you know in the game or on this page. The Moonstream Tech Privacy Policy applies to all our games and complements this policy.
