Our games & apps

Games that keep opening up.

Boards that grow. Rooms that unlock. Ponds to hop across. Grills to fire up and gardens to grow. Games built to be played your way — no account required.

  • Last Tile

    Merge · Survival · Puzzle

    Out now on Google Play

    A calm but addictive merge puzzle. Start on a small grid, merge matching tiles, and unlock a larger board. Survive as long as you can while keeping your combo chain alive. Free to play, with optional rewarded ads and a global leaderboard.

    Get it on Google Play Visit Last Tile Support Privacy

  • Mozai

    Color · Pixel · Calm

    Coming to Google Play

    A calm pixel coloring game. Pick a color and discover the picture — correct cells fill in, wrong taps do nothing. No numbers, no pressure. Finish pictures to unlock new rooms. Free to play, with optional rewarded ads.

    Visit Mozai Support Privacy Terms

  • Ribbo Pond Hopper

    Hop · Collect · Compete

    In development

    Hop across the pond with Ribbo. Play Classic or Time Attack, dress Ribbo up with colors, hats and costumes, and chase your best score on the leaderboard.

    Visit Ribbo Support Privacy

  • My Mini BBQ

    Grill · Serve · Grow

    In development

    An arcade idle BBQ tycoon. Grill burgers, serve the cars lining up at your window, hire a crew and grow a tiny hut into a busy kitchen. Free to play, with ads.

    Visit My Mini BBQ Support Privacy

  • My Tiny Garden

    Plant · Harvest · Sell

    In development

    A cozy gardening game. Grow vegetables, fruit and flowers, sell your harvest at the stand, bargain with customers and decorate every corner. Free to play, with ads.

    Visit My Tiny Garden Support Privacy

  • MeshChat

    App · Private · Serverless

    Coming to Google Play

    Private one-to-one messaging with no servers. End-to-end encrypted, no phone number or e-mail, and messages that delete themselves after 30 days. Free, with no ads.

    Visit MeshChat Support Privacy Terms
