About the game

Last Tile is a merge puzzle about survival. Every merge is a turn, the board keeps opening up as you climb, and the run only ends when no matching pair is left.

Plan ahead, react fast, survive longer. No timers, no energy systems, no paywalls — just you and the board. Free to play, with optional rewarded ads.

Quick sessions.
Deep strategy.

  • 01

    A board that grows.

    Start on a 5×5 grid. Hit value milestones to open the frame — 7×7, 9×9 and beyond.

  • 02

    Hazards every run.

    Fire, Ice and Poison tiles show up where they hurt most, so no two runs play the same.

  • 03

    Shields when it gets tight.

    Clear a hazard and keep the tile’s value. Out of shields? Watch a short optional video to earn three more.

  • 04

    Combos that stack.

    Chain merges back to back to build your multiplier and push your score higher.

  • 05

    Global leaderboard.

    Climb the global ranks — and keep your best ten runs on your own device.

  • 06

    Every move is a choice.

    Merge, split, slide or swap. Think a few turns ahead and the board will reward you.

How to play

Easy to learn.
Hard to put down.

  1. 01

    Two adjacent tiles with the same value can be merged. The result is the next tier (e.g. two 4s become an 8).

  2. 02

    Each merge counts as a turn; a new tile spawns somewhere on the board, or an existing tile is infected by a hazard.

  3. 03

    Use the Shield counter to clean Fire, Ice or Poison from a tile and keep its value.

  4. 04

    Reach milestone values to unlock additional cells and eventually grow the active region.

  5. 05

    If no two adjacent tiles match anywhere on the board, the run ends — your score is recorded on the local leaderboard and on the global leaderboard.
