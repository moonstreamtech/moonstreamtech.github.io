About the game

Mozai is a color-by-number game. Every artwork is split into little tiles, every tile has a number and every number has a color. Pick a color, fill in its tiles and watch the picture come to life.

Over 400 hand-made artworks wait in 7 levels, from Beginner to Masterpiece, with special collections of famous paintings, world wonders, space and the sea. It is designed to be calm — no timers, no accounts, progress saved on your phone. Free to play, with ads and optional purchases.

Core features

6 reasons to play

  • 01

    Paint by numbers.

    Every tile has a number and every number a color. Pick a color, fill its tiles and watch the picture come to life.

  • 02

    400+ artworks, 7 levels.

    From cute Beginner pictures to big Masterpiece scenes, plus famous paintings, world wonders, space and the sea.

  • 03

  • 06

How to play

Easy to learn.
Hard to put down.

  1. 01

    Pick a color from the palette at the bottom of the screen. Its number shows which tiles it paints.

  2. 02

    Tap a tile with that number, or slide your finger to paint many at once. Hold a tile to pick its color.

  3. 03

    Each color counts the tiles still to paint, and gets a check mark when it is done — you always know what's left.

  4. 04

    Finish the artwork to hang it on your collection wall, earn XP and open new levels and special artworks.
