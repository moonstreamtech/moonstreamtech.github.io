Who can use MeshChat

You must be at least 18 years old to use MeshChat. By using it you confirm that you are.

Moonstream Tech grants you a personal, non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable license to install and use the App on devices you own or control, for personal, non-commercial use.

Acceptable use

You are responsible for what you send. You must not use MeshChat to:

  • Create, share or request child sexual abuse material, or sexualise, groom or exploit minors in any way. We have zero tolerance for this — see our Child Safety Standards.
  • Harass, bully, threaten, intimidate or incite violence or hatred against anyone.
  • Send spam, scams, phishing or unsolicited bulk messages.
  • Impersonate another person or organisation, or mislead others about who you are.
  • Share someone's private information, or intimate images, without their consent.
  • Share content that is illegal, or that infringes someone else's rights.
  • Spread malware, or attack, overload or interfere with the network or other users' devices.

Reporting and blocking

You can block anyone, and report a user or a message from the chat menu (… → Şikayet et) or by e-mailing moonstreamtech@gmail.com. Because messages are end-to-end encrypted and MeshChat has no servers, we cannot read conversations or remove content from other people's devices; we rely on the information in your report. We review every report and, where the law requires or where a child may be at risk, we pass it to the competent authorities.

Your account and keys

Your account is a key pair stored on your device. You are responsible for keeping your key file, recovery code and optional password safe. Moonstream Tech does not have them and cannot recover an account whose keys are lost.

Your content

You keep all rights to what you send. Messages and photos are deleted automatically 30 days after they are sent, and accounts not used for 90 days are deleted. The App is not a storage service; keep copies of anything important.
