Moonstream Tech/MeshChat/Child Safety Standards

Child Safety Standards

Zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation.

Last updated: 4 October 2026

On this page

What is prohibited How to report What we do Compliance Contact

Moonstream Tech prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM) on MeshChat, without exception. These are our published standards.

What is prohibited

  • Creating, sharing, requesting or storing child sexual abuse material of any kind.
  • Grooming: contacting or building a relationship with a minor for sexual purposes.
  • Sexualising minors, sextortion, and trafficking or soliciting minors.
  • Any other content or behaviour that exploits or endangers children.

MeshChat is for adults (18+). Accounts used for any of the above violate our Terms of Use.

How to report

  • In the app: open the chat → … → Şikayet et (Report), or long-press a message → Şikayet et. Choose "Çocukların güvenliğini tehdit eden içerik". You can block the person at the same time.
  • By e-mail: moonstreamtech@gmail.com — please include the person's name#tag and, if possible, their account ID (shown in their profile).
  • If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local police first. In Turkey you can also report online at ihbarweb.org.tr; elsewhere, the NCMEC CyberTipline or your national hotline (INHOPE).

What we do

MeshChat has no servers and messages are end-to-end encrypted, so we cannot see conversations or delete content from users' devices. When we receive a report we:

  • review it promptly, with priority for anything involving a child;
  • report apparent CSAM and child exploitation to the competent authorities and to NCMEC, as required by law, together with the information in the report;
  • cooperate with law enforcement requests to the extent we are able (we hold no message content or user data);
  • keep the app's protections up to date: blocking, reporting, and adult-only use.

Compliance

We comply with applicable child safety laws, including reporting obligations, in the countries where MeshChat is available.

Child safety contact

Our designated point of contact for child safety matters is moonstreamtech@gmail.com (Moonstream Tech).
